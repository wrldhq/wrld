📋 Community Guidelines

Learning comes first. These guidelines protect that.

Every WRLD member agrees to these before posting for the first time. They exist to keep this a safe, welcoming space for young people — not to slow anyone down.

1. Every contribution is tied to a real account

WRLD does not support anonymous posting. Posting or replying requires a verified account, a completed Adulting Readiness Assessment, and at least one completed Playbook — this keeps conversations grounded in people who actually know what WRLD is about.

2. Be kind, always

No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or targeting anyone based on who they are. Disagreement is fine — cruelty isn't.

3. Protect everyone's privacy

Don't share your own personal contact information or anyone else's (phone numbers, addresses, financial details). WRLD's automated screening flags this on sight.

4. No unsafe advice, spam, or scams

This includes dangerous advice, self-harm encouragement, phishing attempts, promotional spam, or "get rich quick" schemes. If you're struggling, WRLD's Playbooks and Orbit can point you toward real support — but this isn't a crisis service, so please also reach out to a local crisis line or someone you trust if you need immediate help.

5. How moderation works

WRLD uses multiple layers to keep things safe:

  • Automated screening — every post is checked for known unsafe patterns before it goes live.
  • Community reporting — anyone can report a post or reply; enough reports automatically hides it pending review.
  • Human review — WRLD Administrators review held and reported content and can approve, remove, warn, or suspend.
  • Progressive trust — new members have modest daily posting limits that grow as they participate positively over time.

6. What happens if these guidelines are broken

Depending on severity: content may be removed, you may receive a warning, or — for repeated or serious violations — your account may be suspended.
