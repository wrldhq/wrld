🌍 Our Mission

Nobody teaches adulthood.

Twelve-plus years of school, and somehow no one sits you down to explain taxes, rent, credit, or how to handle a bad interview. WRLD exists to close that gap — with practical, free, judgment-free lessons for the parts of life school leaves out.

Why WRLD Exists

School prepares you for exams. It doesn't prepare you for Tuesday.

Most school systems are built to teach subjects, not life. You can graduate knowing calculus and never once have practiced reading a lease, building a budget, or negotiating a starting salary. You can be accepted into a great university and have no idea how financial aid actually works, or how to manage a first bout of homesickness and anxiety without a script.

This isn't anyone's fault in particular — it's a structural gap. Curricula are built around standardized outcomes, and "how to function as an adult" doesn't fit neatly into a semester or a test. So it gets left out, over and over, for generation after generation, and everyone just has to learn it the hard way — usually through an expensive mistake or an uncomfortable surprise.

We think young people deserve better than trial and error for something this important.

Our Vision

A world where nobody has to learn adulthood alone.

Not everyone grows up with someone to call when a lease looks confusing, a paycheck doesn't stretch far enough, or a first job feels overwhelming. We want WRLD to be that steady, judgment-free voice for anyone who needs one — regardless of where they start from.

Everything on WRLD — every Playbook, program, and worksheet — is free, and built to actually be used, not just read once and forgotten.

Meet the Founder

Where WRLD actually came from.

🧑‍💻
Desiree Yhap
Founder, WRLD

When I was planning my future, I realized school had taught me how to succeed academically — but very little about navigating adulthood. I found myself searching across YouTube, Reddit, blogs, websites, podcasts, and social media, trying to piece together practical life advice from a dozen different places at once.

I was fortunate to grow up with an incredibly supportive family who always encouraged me and shared their knowledge whenever they could. But I also realized that even they had to learn many of life's biggest lessons entirely through trial and error — because a resource like WRLD simply didn't exist when they needed one.

That made me realize how many people don't have that same support system. Many young people are trying to navigate adulthood completely on their own, without anyone to ask the questions that feel too basic to ask out loud.

WRLD was created to become the resource I wish everyone had access to. Its purpose is to bring practical knowledge into one place — while allowing people to learn not only from educational resources, but also from one another.

Knowledge shouldn't stay with one person. It should be shared.

So — welcome. I'm genuinely glad you're here. Whether you're figuring out your first lease, your first paycheck, or just what's next, I hope WRLD becomes the resource that makes it a little easier. Come learn with us, and maybe one day, share what you know with someone else too. 🌍

How We're Different

Not another scattered pile of tabs and bookmarks.

Most practical life advice today is scattered across YouTube, Reddit threads, blog posts, and social media — helpful, but disconnected. WRLD is built to be the opposite of that.

😵‍💫 The scattered way

  • 🔍 Piecing advice together from dozens of unrelated sources
  • ❓ No way to know if advice is complete, current, or trustworthy
  • 📼 One-off videos or posts with no structured next step
  • 💸 Paywalls on the more polished, structured courses
  • 🙋 Learning alone, with no community working through the same thing

🌍 The WRLD way

  • 📚 One structured library — Playbooks that build on each other
  • 🧭 Guided Learning Paths, so you always know the next step
  • 📝 Real worksheets, quizzes, and reflection — not just reading
  • 🆓 Completely free, permanently — no paywalled "premium" tier
  • 🤝 Built to grow into a place to learn from each other, not just from us

💙 Why We Chose Free

Why WRLD will always strive to remain free.

WRLD wasn't created to sell courses. It was created because too many young people are expected to figure out adulthood through trial and error.

We believe learning how to budget, apply for scholarships, communicate professionally, navigate university, understand taxes, build healthy habits, and prepare for a career shouldn't depend on someone's ability to pay.

Every Playbook, worksheet, template, and live learning session is offered free because education shouldn't stop where school ends.

Keeping WRLD free is only possible because of volunteers, mentors, partners, and supporters who believe the next generation deserves better. If WRLD has helped you, or if you simply believe this mission matters, we'd be grateful for your support.
