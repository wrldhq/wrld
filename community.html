🔒 Why there's no private messaging

WRLD keeps every conversation public and moderated on purpose. Private messages are much harder to keep safe, so all communication happens through public discussions, Playbook Q&A, Live Learning, and Office Hours instead.

🤝 Mentors

Real people, giving back on their own time

WRLD's Mentorship Network is a volunteer community — anyone can become a Mentor, and every session is hosted by someone doing this because they want to.

Become a Mentor

Mentors host free, live Learning sessions and (soon) 1:1 matched conversations. Here's what to expect:

  • Commitment: As little as one live session — no minimum hours required.
  • Application: Submit an application through Become a Mentor — a real person on the WRLD team reviews it and approves Mentor access by hand.
  • Areas of expertise: Anything covered by a WRLD Program — career, finance, school, wellness, leadership, and more.
  • Tools: Once approved, Mentor Studio lets you schedule sessions and set up your public mentor profile.
